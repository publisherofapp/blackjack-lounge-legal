Delete Your Account

You can request the permanent deletion of your Blackjack Lounge account and all associated data at any time. Please read the steps and information below carefully before proceeding.

📋

How to Request Account Deletion

1
Send a deletion request email

Email us at publisherofapp@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Blackjack Lounge Account".

2
Include your account details

Provide the email address linked to your Google account used to sign in to Blackjack Lounge, or your in-game username so we can locate your account.

3
We'll confirm and process your request

You'll receive a confirmation email within 7 business days. Your account and associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.

🗑

What Data Gets Deleted

Data Type Action Retention Period
Account profile (name, avatar) Deleted Within 30 days
Game history & statistics Deleted Within 30 days
Chip & diamond balance Deleted Within 30 days
In-app messages & inbox Deleted Within 30 days
Friends list & friend codes Deleted Within 30 days
FCM notification token Deleted Within 30 days
Purchase records (IAP) Retained Up to 7 years (legal/tax requirement)
Device ID (ban records) Retained Retained to prevent re-registration abuse
⚠️

This action is permanent and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your username, chip balance, purchased card decks, diamond balance, game history, and all progress cannot be recovered under any circumstances.

💡

Before You Delete

→

→
